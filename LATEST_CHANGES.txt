================================================================================
  HOW TO USE ROLLBACK
================================================================================

STEP 1: View Promotion History
  1. Open: student-promotions.html
  2. Scroll down to: "Promotion History & Rollback" section
  3. See list of recent 20 promotions

STEP 2: Find Promotion to Rollback
  1. Look for the promotion in the table
  2. See: Student Name | Roll No | From Class | To Class | Date
  3. Click: Red "Rollback" button (<- icon)

STEP 3: Confirm Rollback
  1. Modal appears with student details
  2. Shows: Student Name & From/To classes
  3. See: Checkbox "I confirm to rollback this promotion"
  4. Check the checkbox
  5. Click: Red "Rollback" button

STEP 4: Success
  1. Modal closes
  2. Green success message: "Promotion rolled back successfully!"
  3. History refreshes
  4. Student moved back to original class ✓

================================================================================
  WHAT ROLLBACK DOES
================================================================================

Before Rollback:
  Student classId: 2nd Year ⬅️
  StudentPromotion record: Exists
  Activity logs: [PROMOTION: 1st Year → 2nd Year]

After Rollback:
  Student classId: 1st Year ✓ (Back to original)
  StudentPromotion record: Still exists (for history)
  Activity logs: [PROMOTION: 1st Year → 2nd Year, ROLLBACK: 2nd Year → 1st Year]

Fee records: ✓ Unchanged (linked by studentId)
Attendance: ✓ Unchanged
Exam results: ✓ Unchanged
Activity trail: ✓ Complete audit log
